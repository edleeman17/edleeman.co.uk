I have a record player which I previously had a raspberry pi connected to which would set up a little local pirate radio for my Sonos system to connect to. It worked great for many years. Well in fact there was nothing actually wrong with it. But I discovered [Quanta](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/quanta-vinyl-companion/id1600262501) which I’m running from an iPad and casting over AirPlay to my speakers. Giving me a simple little UI. But also, a killer ability to cast to my AirPods.

Sweet sweet vinyl crackles in my ears from anywhere in the house.
