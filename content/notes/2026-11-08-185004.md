Rookie mistake with the DIY antenna. Well any antenna I guess. Just a rookie error.

Stuck the antenna in the loft getting S9 on the radio. Turns out you’re supposed to measure the strength of the signal when receiving something. I was measuring the noise level (not receiving).

So basically, I installed my antenna in the worst place possible in the loft. 

Still managed to hit the [ISS repeater](https://issfanclub.eu/iss-frequencies/) though! 

So two things to do now. Muck about in the loft trying to find the best position. And also muck about with the electrics to track down the source of the noise.
