# Streams of consciousness ~ 14th September 2026

I need to spend some more time in my head and figured I would pick up journaling again. But I also thought, it would be good to put all of conscious onto the internet (probably not a good idea, but let’s call it art.)

I want to document not just the thoughts I’m having at this moment. But also the environment that I’m having them in. Looking back at journal entries only gives you some of the picture. I want visual representation as well as text.

This is an experiment. It probably won’t last, but for it not to last - I have to at least start it.

Listening: [alt-J - An Awesome Wave](https://album.link/i/1622205752)

—-

> I feel like I’m being driven by something out of my control
>
> I feel like I have lost the ability to think for myself, especially with AI.
>
> I have lost the very thing that got me through the day, which was solving problems.
>
> The use of AI, I think has just enhanced my dependency on dopamine. I never realised it until I stopped using my brain. I’ve stopped vaping too which is making it really hard to get anything done.
>
> My anxiety is a lot at the moment. I’m on enough sertraline to numb everything yet this also feels like part of the problem with the autopilot.
>
> I’m losing the passion for the things that I love. The self doubt is setting in with photography. I’m just not happy with what I shoot any more. I need a win.
>
> Self image is also kicking me. Thinking about complete wardrobe redesigns. Seeing what other people wear.
>
> But the issue is, they all have beards. Which I can’t grow. They also wear trousers and heavy cotton items. None of which I am comfortable with, in both senses of the word. Which is why I stick to black shorts and T-shirts. Even socks are too much.
>
> I’m on a weird digital nomad hype at the moment where I’m wanting to give tech a big “fuck you”
>
> Passion for self hosting is back
>
> I’m reading again
>
> Trying to get my daughters offline with the likes of retro tv shows
>
> All of which are huge hype at the moment. I know I am being influenced down this rabbit hole.
>
> So not only do I not have to think anymore. But every thought I do have has been predetermined and fabricated.
>
> But at least if that fabrication is moving away from the machines, then surely that can only be a good thing!
