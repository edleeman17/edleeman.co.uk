# Stopping the vapes 

I’ve been trying to quit vaping for over a year now. Cold turkey doesn’t work, I always fall back into it - even after a month vape-free I fall back into it so easily. All it takes is one bad day.

I think the hardest part is having to stop something I really enjoy. It helps me think and stay focused, it makes me feel ‘normal’. I don’t feel human without it. 

But I know deep down that I would feel human without all these dopamine hits, once the addiction has worn off. All I’m doing is wasting money. 

£12 a week isn’t a lot. I spend more on other things, but when it contributes absolutely nothing to my life, it’s a bit harder to justify (excluding the enjoyment of it I guess).

So what’s changed?

![4c184658 eb6e 430e 95b6 62aa266444f8](https://cdn.charityshot.co.uk/web/4c184658-eb6e-430e-95b6-62aa266444f8.jpg)

I’ve bought a… hang on, I’ll get the full title. I’ve bought a “Breathing Necklace to Replace Bad Habit with 3pcs Refillable Mint Pods, Habit Support Necklace for Stress Relief Sensory Fidget for Adults” thing. £10 on Amazon. 

It’s a little tube which you just suck on, it has the restrictive airflow which you would get from a vape, but it also has the ability to insert these filter-like things which give off a slight flavour.

Now I’m no bullshitter, these are nothing like a vape. But I think they’re _enough_. 

Enough to take my mind off it at least. The throat hit you get from the mint flavour is just enough. I’ve got a ‘strawberry ice’ one at the moment but it may as well just be ‘ice’ or ‘dry filter’ flavour. I think they forgot to introduce the strawberry.

It comes on a chain which I hang round my neck. It’s close to me on purpose. There’s absolutely zero friction to using it and therefore I have no excuse not to.

I’m on day 3 now of zero nicotine. I’m struggling, I’m not going to lie. But this thing is making it easier. You can follow my progress on my [/since](/since) page. I need to reset the counter though.
